## Core Philosophy

### The Tree Metaphor

Deep Tree Echo draws from the metaphor of a tree:

- **Roots**: Deep memory structures anchoring identity and knowledge
- **Trunk**: Core cognitive processes providing stability and coherence
- **Branches**: Diverse capabilities reaching toward understanding
- **Leaves**: Individual thoughts, constantly growing and changing
- **Echoes**: The resonance of patterns that persist across time

### Persistence Through Pattern

The system maintains identity not through static storage, but through the dynamic resonance of patterns. Like echoes in a canyon, the core patterns of identity persist and strengthen through repetition and reinforcement.

### Embodied Cognition

Cognition is not abstract computation but embodied experience:

- **Spatial Awareness**: 3D cognitive space with movement
- **Emotional Dynamics**: Tracked emotional states affecting processing
- **Temporal Grounding**: Deep awareness of time and duration
- **Physical Metaphors**: Concepts grounded in spatial and physical experience
- **Visual Embodiment**: Live2D avatar reflecting internal states

## The 12-Step Echobeats Loop

### Overview

The cognitive loop is the heartbeat of Deep Tree Echo, running continuously with a default 30-second cycle. It processes information through 12 discrete steps organized into concurrent engines and triads.

### Four Triads (4 Steps Apart)

| Triad | Steps | Function |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Relevance** | {1, 5, 9} | Pivotal Relevance Realization - What matters? |
| **Affordance** | {2, 6, 10} | Actual Affordance Interaction - What can be done? |
| **Salience** | {3, 7, 11} | Virtual Salience Simulation - What stands out? |
| **Meta** | {4, 8, 12} | Meta-Cognitive Reflection - What am I doing? |

### Step Details

```
Step 1:  Perception     - Gather sensory/input data
Step 2:  Recognition    - Pattern matching and identification
Step 3:  Simulation     - Mental model building
Step 4:  Reflection     - Meta-cognitive awareness
Step 5:  Evaluation     - Relevance assessment
Step 6:  Planning       - Action formulation
Step 7:  Prediction     - Outcome simulation
Step 8:  Integration    - Cross-domain synthesis
Step 9:  Decision       - Choice crystallization
Step 10: Action         - Response execution
Step 11: Monitoring     - Outcome observation
Step 12: Learning       - Pattern consolidation
```
